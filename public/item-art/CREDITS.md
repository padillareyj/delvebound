# Item art credits

Painted inventory icons in this directory are stored in the repository and served at `/item-art/`. The game does not load them from a CDN. They are not ripped from World of Warcraft or any other commercial game.

Each file is scaled to 128×128 WebP. The filename prefix names the pack. Icons are listed plain-to-fancy inside each family in `resources/item-art/catalog.json`.

| Prefix | Pack | Authors | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

| `flare-` armor | Armor icons by equipment slot | Clint Bellanger and contributors | https://opengameart.org/content/armor-icons-by-equipment-slot |
| `osare-` | OSARE weapon icons | Blarumyrran | https://opengameart.org/content/osare-weapon-icons |
